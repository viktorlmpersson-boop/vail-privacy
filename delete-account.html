Delete Your Account & Data

Last updated: April 2026

You can permanently delete your Vail account and all associated data at any time. Deletion is instant and irreversible.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your profile (name, age, gender, preferences)
  • All voice recordings you have uploaded
  • Your profile photo
  • Your match history and all messages
  • Your location data
  • Your account credentials

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Your matches will lose access to your voice recordings and messages.

How to Delete Your Account

The fastest way is directly inside the app:

  1. Open the Vail app
  2. Go to the Profile tab (bottom right)
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

Your account and all data will be permanently removed immediately.

Can't Access the App?

If you are unable to log in or access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us at:
privacy@vailapp.com

Include the email address associated with your Vail account. We will process your request and confirm deletion within 7 days.
